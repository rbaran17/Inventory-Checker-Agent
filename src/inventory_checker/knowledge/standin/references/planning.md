# Planning the inventory observation
> refs: AU-C 501; ISA 501; AS 2510
> topics: planning

Plan attendance based on the risks of material misstatement for inventory, the nature and locations of
inventory, internal control over inventory, and the client's count procedures. Planning matters include:
timing of the count, which locations to attend, obtaining and reviewing the count instructions in advance,
obtaining the perpetual listing to select sheet-to-floor items, the number of observers needed, any
specialists (for example to measure bulk materials), and agreement with the client on photographing and
recording the count.

# Setting tolerance for count differences
> refs: AU-C 530; ISA 530; AS 2315
> topics: planning, sampling, test_counts

The threshold above which a test count difference is investigated and reported is an engagement team
judgement under firm methodology, informed by materiality and the nature of the items. Document the
threshold chosen and the basis for it before the count.
