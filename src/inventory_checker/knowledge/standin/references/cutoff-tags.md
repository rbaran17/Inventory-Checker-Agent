# Receiving and shipping cutoff at the count
> refs: AU-C 501; ISA 501; AS 2510
> topics: cutoff

Record details of the last receiving and shipping documents used before the count (document number and
date) and observe that goods received or shipped after cutoff are treated consistently. These details
are later compared with the accounting records to test that purchases, sales and inventory are recorded
in the correct period.

# Tag control
> refs: AU-C 501; ISA 501; AS 2510
> topics: tag_control

Where pre-numbered tags or count sheets are used, record the range issued, and at the end of the count
the tags used, unused and voided. This lets the auditor later check that no tags were added, lost or
altered after the count, and that the final inventory listing contains every used tag and only those.
