# Attendance at the physical inventory count
> refs: ISA 501.4; AU-C 501; AS 2510
> topics: planning, procedures

When inventory is material, the auditor attends the physical count, unless that is impracticable, to
obtain evidence about the existence and condition of inventory. While attending, the auditor evaluates
management's instructions and procedures for recording and controlling the count, observes how the
count procedures are carried out, inspects the inventory, and performs test counts. The auditor then
performs procedures over the entity's final inventory records to check that they reflect the actual
count results.

# Count at a date other than the financial statement date
> refs: ISA 501.5; AU-C 501; AS 2510
> topics: planning, alternative_procedures

If the count happens at a date other than the period end, the auditor also performs procedures to
obtain evidence that changes in inventory between the count date and the period end are properly
recorded (a roll-forward or roll-back). How much testing is needed depends on the strength of controls
over inventory movements and perpetual records.

# Unforeseen circumstances preventing attendance
> refs: ISA 501.6; AU-C 501
> topics: alternative_procedures

If unforeseen circumstances stop the auditor attending the planned count, the auditor makes or observes
some physical counts on an alternative date and performs procedures over the intervening transactions.
