# Inspecting the condition of inventory
> refs: ISA 501.4(a)(iii); AU-C 501; AS 2510
> topics: condition, procedures

While attending the count, inspect inventory for evidence of damage, obsolescence or slow movement
(for example dust, expired dates, damaged packaging, items in quarantine areas). Record the items,
locations and approximate quantities. These observations feed the auditor's work on inventory
valuation and net realisable value.

# Inventory held for or by third parties
> refs: ISA 501.8; AU-C 501
> topics: third_party, condition

Goods on the premises that belong to others should be identified and excluded from the count. When the
entity's inventory is held by a third party and is material, the auditor obtains evidence of its
existence and condition by confirmation from the third party and/or inspection or other procedures.
